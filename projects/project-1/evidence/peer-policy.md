# Peer policy frozen before calculations

Written 2026-10-19 during disclosed AI-assisted Edition B research. This is an AI-assisted policy, not a reconstruction of a human AI-off Week 4 policy. Economic comparison date: 2026-08-27 US market close.

Admit listed US operating software issuers with a predominantly recurring subscription model, enterprise customer exposure, positive latest publicly available annual GAAP earnings, USD reporting, and auditable share/split definitions. Exclude banks, hardware-dominated businesses, private companies, the target itself, duplicate securities and loss-making issuers from P/E. Do not select based on the resulting multiple. Limit the investigated set to two relevant candidates.

Investigate Adobe and ServiceNow. Adobe has customer-experience software overlap but substantial creative/prosumer exposure; qualify it as a mature subscription reference. ServiceNow has enterprise workflow subscriptions but substantially faster growth; qualify it as an upper growth reference. Do not treat either as an interchangeable Salesforce clone. Microsoft is excluded because consolidated cloud infrastructure, operating systems and productivity economics make a direct application-software multiple difficult to interpret; no Microsoft calculation will be used.

Use the same 2026-08-27 US share closing-price date. Use the latest annual GAAP revenue and GAAP diluted EPS public at that date, with fiscal ends stated. Use latest public balance-sheet financial debt and cash/marketable securities for EV, with those dates visible. EV = price times period-end ordinary shares + financial debt - cash and marketable debt securities. Treat operating leases as operating costs; do not add lease liabilities to EV. Exclude strategic equity investments consistently from peer EV adjustments and label the residual contamination; show Salesforce strategic investments separately in the DCF bridge. Do not mix a provider EV with our custom denominator. P/E = price / annual GAAP diluted EPS; no cash/debt bridge on P/E. Exclude nonpositive inputs loudly.

Report every admitted result, min/median/max and leave-one-out; a one-peer result is a reference, not a range. Show target historical P/E separately because investment gains contaminate it. Do not average comps with the DCF. Differences in growth, stock compensation, investment gains, buybacks and fiscal ends must qualify the conclusion.
