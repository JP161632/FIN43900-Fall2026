# Comparable-company policy - retained before selection/calculation

The policy in the September10 Lab8 writeup predates this October19 rebuild. Its original path/hash/commit are in prior-lab-index.json; this restatement does not fabricate a newly backdated policy or an AI-off checkpoint.

Include profitable US-listed fabless semiconductor designers with outsourced manufacturing, USD reporting and meaningful accelerated/data-center exposure. Exclude foundries, equipment, memory pure plays, loss-making firms and financial issuers. Select by business/financial comparability before seeing implied valuation. NVIDIA is the subject, not a member of its own peer median.

AMD: use with qualification for lower margins, CPUs/client/gaming and smaller AI share. Broadcom: qualify because of custom silicon/networking and substantial infrastructure software; not a pure GPU competitor. TSMC excluded: foundry capital intensity. ASML excluded: equipment. Micron excluded: memory manufacturing/cycle. Intel excluded: owned fabs and comparability/profitability issues. These exclusions are policy decisions, not claims about current prices.

All prices: Sep10 unadjusted US close. Equity multiple = price / latest completed annual GAAP total diluted EPS. EV multiple = (price * latest-public-quarter GAAP diluted-share proxy + carrying financial debt - cash - marketable debt securities) / latest completed annual revenue. Operating leases remain operating consistently. These are disclosed proxy EVs, not vendor EVs; exact current cap tables, debt fair values, minority interests and investment realizations are not assumed known. No material preferred/minority claim identified in these supplied balance sheets; refresh before investment use.

Annual denominators deliberately reproduce Lab8's consistent historical GAAP convention, despite differing fiscal year ends; they are not labeled forward/TTM. Latest financial balances avoid ignoring new leverage. To backsolve NVIDIA equity from the peer EV/sales proxy, add only its latest cash and debt securities and subtract its BOOK financial debt, exactly matching the comparison numerator. Noncash investment holdings stay inside this broad comparison EV for every name; do not add NVIDIA's investments a second time. NVIDIA's DCF bridge instead values operating assets, investments and debt separately; the two conventions are reconciled, not silently equated. Broadcom software and AMD acquisition accounting make either peer endpoint an unreliable intrinsic target. Exclude one peer and the median becomes the other peer: the full interval must remain visible. Do not mechanically average with DCF.

Result: weak contextual cross-check only. The wider multiples range reflects growth expectations, fiscal lag, margin mix and accounting, while DCF identifies which demand/return assumptions price would require. A stable larger qualified sample or segment valuations could change the weight given to comparisons.
