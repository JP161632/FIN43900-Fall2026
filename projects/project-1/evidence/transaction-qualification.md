# Transaction evidence - context and exclusion

Hugging Face agreement disclosed September3 2026: [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000078/nvda-20260902.htm), expected closing first half2027. Approximately11.9bn stockholder consideration plus up to1bn equity retention. The [issuer blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/) cites12.9303bn headline total. Retention compensates future service; it is not interchangeable with equity purchase price. Public audited acquired EBITDA, consistent standalone revenue and net debt are not supplied in this evidence set. No transaction multiple is calculated from press estimates.

Control of an open-model platform, developer distribution and strategic benefits differ from buying NVIDIA's public minority shares. Proposed transaction, unclosed regulatory risk, integration/retention and missing audited denominators make it unsuitable as a direct price-per-revenue benchmark. Do not turn the headline into an operating-company EV multiple or assume synergy.

The model nevertheless recognizes potential funding: assume11.9bn cash purchase in year1, up to1bn retention replaced with cash compensation, no incremental acquired cash flow. Acquisition cash is separate CFI/bridge, retention in operating expense. All purchase assets are temporarily displayed in unallocated goodwill; this is an explicit simplified forecast, not a GAAP allocation. Update on financing/closing/standalone evidence. Cancellation removes these costs; it does not prove intrinsic undervaluation. Groq non-exclusive licensing is also unsuitable as a control-transaction EV precedent.
