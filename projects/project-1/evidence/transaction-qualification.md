# Transaction qualification

Investigated transaction: Salesforce / Informatica, definitive agreement announced May27 2025; control acquisition, closed in November2025 and already inside Salesforce's historical consolidation at this valuation cutoff.

Primary evidence: [Informatica merger 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1868778/000119312525128808/d930092d8k.htm), [joint issuer announcement](https://sst-www.informatica.com.edgekey.net/content/informatica-www/en_us/about-us/news/news-releases/2025/05/20250527-salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-informatica.html), and Salesforce FY2026 10-K acquisition note (`CRM_K`). Announcement evidence was public2025-05-27; final purchase accounting was public2026-03-02; retrieved2026-10-19.

The announcement describes approximately8 billion dollars of **equity value net of Salesforce's existing investment**, not a clean enterprise-value numerator. The merger bought control and included strategic integration potential. Salesforce's final acquisition accounting contains different consideration/PPA concepts. Neither can be divided casually by a public-comparable revenue figure and called a standalone EV/revenue multiple.

Disposition: qualify as evidence of strategic relevance and acquisition-accounting contamination; **exclude from a numeric transaction-implied CRM range**. No control premium or synergy value is transferred to a minority common share. The professor's requirement is conditional on defensible control/synergy/time context; refusing an inconsistent transaction multiple is an explained policy decision, not a missing invented number.

Contentful and Fin are pending at the cutoff, not completed public trading comparables. Their combined disclosed cash consideration5100M is used only in an adverse cash-cost/no-benefit pro-forma stress. Without dependable stand-alone revenue, debt/cash, PPA, control context and attributable synergies, no transaction multiple is fabricated.
